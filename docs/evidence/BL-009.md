## KI-002: offline worrying-notes check

Measured 2026-11-20 on invented notes. "Before" is the one-line regex in
`public/app.js` from BL-001; "after" is `public/safety.js`.

| Set | Check | Worrying notes caught | False alarms on ordinary days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test list (`test/safety-cases.js`, 44 + 37) | before | 18/44 (26 missed) | 7/37 |
| Test list | after | **44/44** | **0/37** |
| Held-out (10 + 10, below, written after the rules) | before | 0/10 | 0/10 |
| Held-out | after | **2/10** | 0/10 |

The test list grew in both review rounds. Round 2 found that the round-1 fix for false
alarms had made the check miss serious notes ("Dad punched my brother", "Dad said he
would hit me"...); those 14 notes are now in the worrying list and caught. Round 1: the reviewer found 10 everyday sentences the
first version wrongly flagged ("Dad kicked my football", "Mum shook her head", "we
visited grandpa in hospital"...). They are now in the ordinary list and pass.

What this means: the new rules fix every miss and false alarm on the list they were
written against, but on fresh wordings they catch only 2 in 10. A phrase list cannot
recognise meaning. KI-002 is improved, not solved. Recommendation in "Follow-ups".
