# BL-001: Hackathon demo, day to plan to interactive story

Kind: autonomous run (compressed for the 2-hour hackathon)
Status: implemented

## Goal
A parent on a phone enters what happened today, approves a story plan, and the child
picks what the hero does at two choice moments, ending with a lesson and questions.

## In scope
FR-01, FR-02, FR-03, FR-05, FR-10..FR-14, FR-20..FR-29, FR-30, FR-32, SR-01..SR-06,
NFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-05, NFR-06.

## Out of scope
Accounts, saved stories, images, audio, voice input, payments.

## Acceptance tests (automated)
BL-001-T01..T15 in `test/`. They cover plan parsing, the unsafe-notes verdict, input
validation, offline mode, approval gating, streaming without the marker, the ending,
error handling, and the completeness of the offline story.

## Human acceptance test
1. Open the app on your phone. You see "Tonight's story" and four heroes.
2. Type a first name, pick an age and a hero, tap "Next".
3. Type what happened (for example "Fought with a friend over a toy car") and tap
   "Make a story plan".
4. Check that a plan appears with a theme, lesson, setting, how it echoes the day and
   two choice moments, and that no real names appear.
5. Type a change (for example "set it in space") and tap "Update plan". The plan changes.
6. Tap "Looks good, write the story". Words start appearing within a few seconds.
7. Pick an option. The story continues from that choice. Tap "Go back and choose
   differently" once and pick another option: the story follows the new choice.
8. Finish the story. You see a calm ending, tonight's lesson and questions to talk about.
9. Go back to the start, write something worrying (for example "a grown-up hurt
   him"), and check that the app does not write a story and suggests talking to
   someone you trust.
10. Without internet or an AI key the badge says "Offline story" and the built-in kite
    story still works end to end.
