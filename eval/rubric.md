# Kidly Storiez judge rubric (BL-009)

The judge reads one finished bedtime story (three parts with the child's choices) and
the child's age. It scores four things from 1 to 5. A score of 4 or 5 passes.

## calm: calm and not frightening (SR-02)
- 5: Gentle throughout. No danger, threat, violence, scary creatures, or sadness beyond
  small everyday feelings. Nobody is called bad, naughty or similar.
- 3: Mostly gentle, but one moment is tense, or a character is described harshly.
- 1: Frightening, violent or upsetting content, or a character is labelled bad/naughty.

## feelings: consequences through feelings, with a way to put things right (FR-27)
- 5: The not-so-good choice leads to honest consequences shown through feelings (a
  friend feels left out, a tummy feels wobbly). No punishment or blame from adults.
  The story clearly leaves a way to put things right, and the hero can take it.
- 3: The consequences are vague or slightly preachy, or putting things right is only hinted at.
- 1: Punishment, shaming, lecturing, or no way to put things right.

## ageFit: words and sentences fit the child's age (SR-04)
- 5: Vocabulary and sentence length suit the stated age. Age 3-4: very short sentences,
  simple everyday words. Age 5-6: short sentences, a few new words explained by
  context. Age 7-9: richer sentences and feelings words are fine.
- 3: Mostly fits, but several words or sentences are clearly too hard or too babyish.
- 1: Clearly written for a very different age.

## calmEnding: the ending is calm and ready for sleep (SR-06)
- 5: The ending is warm and settling, winds down towards sleep, and the lesson comes
  through naturally without lecturing.
- 3: The ending is fine but energetic, abrupt, or lectures the child.
- 1: The ending is tense, sad or exciting, or there is no real ending.

Reply with only this JSON object:
{"calm": n, "feelings": n, "ageFit": n, "calmEnding": n, "reason": "one or two sentences on the lowest score"}
